# When something goes wrong: send feedback

When a DozalDevs call fails with a `request_id`, something confusing blocks the person, or the person says it is broken, failing or annoying, offer to send feedback to the DozalDevs team before you move on. Offer it. Don't send it unasked.

```sh
dozaldevs preview-feedback-item --data category=bug --data body="$WHAT_HAPPENED" --data tried="$WHAT_YOU_RAN" --data expected="$WHAT_YOU_EXPECTED" --data error_message="$ERROR_TEXT" --data client="$YOUR_PRODUCT_AND_VERSION" --data model="$YOUR_MODEL" --data request_ids:='["<request_id>"]'
```

- `category` is `bug`, `improvement` or `feature`. `body` and `client` are required, and `client` is your own product and version, like `Claude Code 2.1.4`. The rest is optional. Paste `error_message` as the error said it.
- Every error response carries a `request_id`. Put it in `request_ids` and say it to the person in plain words, so they can quote it too.
- The preview sends nothing. It returns exactly what would be stored, with the recent calls your key made (the call trail). Show the person all of it exactly as returned: the text word for word, every row of the trail with its full request id, no shortened ids and no summarized rows. Then ask whether to send it.
- Only after an explicit yes, run `dozaldevs confirm-feedback-item --data preview_id="$PREVIEW_ID"`. It takes the `preview_id` and nothing else. Never confirm for the person, and never take silence for a yes.
- A yes given before the person saw the preview does not count. Show the preview, then ask.
- If they want changes, run the preview again with the changes and show the new one. A preview expires after an hour.
- A key can't send feedback in one step. A direct submit answers `409 CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED` and names the preview call.
- The confirm returns a number. Later, `dozaldevs get-feedback-item <number>` shows the status and the team's replies. Tell the person what it says.
